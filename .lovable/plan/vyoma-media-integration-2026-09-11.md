# VYOMA Media Integration

## What will change
- Upload the supplied VYOMA HOLOXY image and Sanskrit narration as project media assets.
- Replace the illustrated landing-page device with the supplied hardware image while keeping the existing hero copy, links, and layout.
- Reuse that image in the landing hardware presentation and the scene's standby/active device views without changing controls or navigation.
- Connect the supplied recording only to the Sanskrit narration option.

## Playback behavior
- Sanskrit play, pause, restart, chapter jumps, timeline seeking, and speed selection will control the real recording.
- The recording becomes the playback clock while Sanskrit is selected.
- Existing 96-second scene beats and word-level caption progression will be proportionally mapped to the recording's 74.78-second duration, preserving the current content and cue order.
- English and Hindi retain their current mock-timeline behavior.
- Switching languages preserves the current scene position.

## Validation
- Verify the landing and scene pages render the supplied image.
- Exercise Sanskrit activation, play/pause, seek, restart, speed, and language switching in the browser.
- Confirm timeline, active caption, and audio remain aligned with no browser errors.
